Modern alarm tones (awaken, sunrise, bounce, gallop, orbit, sway, drip, nudge, wag,
argon, platinum, neon, oxygen, krypton, osmium, rooster, buzzer) come from the
Android Open Source Project, frameworks/base/data/sounds:
  https://github.com/aosp-mirror/platform_frameworks_base/tree/main/data/sounds

Modifications for RiseUp: converted from Ogg Vorbis to 32 kHz mono 16-bit WAV,
loudness-normalised (EBU R128, -16 LUFS); awaken, sway and platinum trimmed to 20 s
with a 0.4 s fade-out. "Rise" was renamed "sunrise".
